10 COPY-PASTE AI PROMPTS FOR RUNNING A SMALL BUSINESS
Works in Claude, ChatGPT or any AI assistant. Replace the [brackets] with your own details.
Privacy tip: never paste customers' phone numbers, addresses, card or bank details. Use first names or "a customer" instead.
Always read the answer before you send it. You decide the price, the promise and the final wording.

1. REPLY TO A REVIEW
Write a polite, short reply from the owner of [shop name] to this customer review. Thank them, address the problem without making excuses, and offer to make it right. Keep it under 60 words.
Review: [paste review]

2. PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Write a clear product description for [product]. Facts: [size, material, price, who it's for]. Keep it simple, 3 short paragraphs, no hype words.

3. WHATSAPP REPLIES YOU REPEAT
I run [type of shop]. Write 10 short WhatsApp replies for the questions customers ask most: price, delivery, opening hours, address, payment, returns. Use friendly, simple English. Leave [brackets] where my details go.

4. SOCIAL POST IDEAS
Give me 7 Instagram post ideas for [type of business] for the next week. For each: a hook line, 2 lines of caption, and a call to action. Mix tips, behind the scenes and customer stories.

5. SALES SUMMARY
Here are this month's sales numbers: [paste numbers]. Summarize in plain words: best sellers, slow movers, and 3 simple actions I can take next month.

6. POLITE PAYMENT REMINDER
Write 3 versions of a polite payment reminder for an invoice of [amount] that is [number] days late: gentle, firm, and final. Keep each under 50 words.

7. JOB AD FOR A HELPER
Write a simple job ad for a [role] at [shop name] in [city]. Include duties, timings, pay range [range], and how to apply by WhatsApp.

8. TURN MY PRICE LIST INTO A MESSAGE
Here is my price list: [paste list]. Turn it into a clean, easy-to-read WhatsApp message I can send to customers.

9. HANDLE A COMPLAINT
A customer says: "[paste complaint]". Suggest 3 calm ways to respond, from simplest to most generous, and tell me what each one costs me.

10. WEEKLY TO-DO PLAN
I own [type of business] with [number] staff. Here is what I need to get done this week: [list]. Put it in a day-by-day plan with the most important task first.
